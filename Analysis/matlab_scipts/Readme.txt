Two folders: 
- 'processing': This script are executed after recording the heart-related potential (HEP). The HEP recording process is described in the paper : Stoffel N., Mouthon M., Yang H., von der Weid L., Concetti C., Blanke O., Aybek A. Attenuated Heartbeat-evoked Potentials in Functional Neurological Disorder (DOI:.... )
				The participant was asked to focus on their heartbeat. The ECG data obtained during this task was then used to calibrate the R-peak detection and timing for the asynchronous condition of the CAS task.
				The 'ECG_R_peak_detection_Compute_Variance_LABVIEW_version_V2.m' script, located in this folder, was designed to perform these tasks. It was executed before the CAS recording. 
- 'analysis': 	Matlab scripts used to analyse the CAS data.
